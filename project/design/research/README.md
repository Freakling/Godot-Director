# Research notes

One dated note per researched topic, written by the research assistant (`/research`, or a design session that delegated a question): `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. Each note leads with its conclusion, lists the options and the sources with a confidence, and says when its claims were verified.

A note records research, never a decision. When you decide after reading one, the decision line in `design/decisions.md` ends with the note's path, so later sessions reuse the evidence. The format and the rules are in `.godot-director/procedures/research.md`.
